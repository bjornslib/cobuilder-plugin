# Product: Kanban board

## Problem

A person who runs CoBuilder wants to know where the work stands. The Work drawer lists each program by its stage and stops there. To learn which epics and slices of a program are planned, in progress, or done, the person opens each program in turn. Nothing shows the work of one program as a board.

## Success metric

A person answers two questions from one page, with no page change except one click: "Which programs are at which stage?" and "What is still open in this program?" We measure it with a browser check on the real bundle. From the board, the person reaches the open epics of any program in one click, and the counts on the board match the record index.

## Announcement — the blog post before the feature

The viewer now has a Kanban board. Open it from the top bar, and each program sits as a card in the column of its stage. Click a card, and the epics of that program sit in three columns: Planned, In progress, and Done. A switch shows the slices in place of the epics. Search, sort, and hide a column, and the address keeps your view, so a link you share opens the same board. A copy icon gives you the prompt that starts the next step for a program or an epic.
