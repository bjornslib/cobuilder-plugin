What this means

The proposer question has an answer for these two programs. On one day, D1 and D2 differ by −0.014, and the interval spans zero. The −0.055 gap on 9 November came mostly from the day, not from the proposer.

Both GEPA prompts help. D1 gains +0.185 and D2 gains +0.171 over the base prompt, both with intervals above zero. The gain is larger on CoBuilder sessions (test set 1) than on the other repositories.

agencheck is the weak spot. D2 scores below D1 there, and neither prompt beats arm A by a clear margin on its 13 stops.

Doubts and open items

  • A second failure mode. Today 2 replies came back empty with finish_reason=stop, after 579 and 862 reasoning tokens. dspy reported "The LM returned an empty or null response". The token limit does not cause this. Each such stop scores 0. The cause is not known.
  • Effect of the limit on scores: not known. No arm moved by more than the spread between days seen before (arm A moved +0.068 between 7 and 9 November).
  • The truncation was mostly in the optimiser runs. The earlier test-set runs had only 0 to 1 truncation warnings each. The 22 warnings were in the GEPA runs, so the next GEPA run is the real test of the new limit.
  • The judge's own replies are not in the call rows. The warning count of 0 includes the judge, but its token use per call is known only as a total.
  • D1 and D2 still differ in two things: the proposer and the reflection model. This run compares the two saved programs, not the two proposers in general.
  • One run per arm. A difference under about 0.07 between days is within noise.
